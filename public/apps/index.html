Device apps

Open this page on the device you're setting up, then tap to download and install. Allow "install unknown apps" when Android asks.

Kiosk Sunmi K2

Locks the device to the self-service menu: fullscreen, home and recents blocked, starts on boot. Staff settings and exit: tap any corner 3 times, or press volume-down 3 times.

Download Kiosk app
  1. Install, open it once.
  2. Tap any corner 3 times → Make this the home app → always.
  3. Tap any corner 3 times → Keep running in background.

Print Agent tills & handhelds

Prints tickets and receipts on a Sunmi built-in printer or a USB printer, even with the app closed and the screen off.

Download Print Agent
  1. Install, open, pick the store and enter the manager PIN.
  2. Tap Print a test slip.
  3. Allow it to run in the background.
Already installed? Installing again updates the app and keeps its settings.
